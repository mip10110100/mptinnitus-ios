# Safety, Scope, and Privacy Guardrails

## Global scope

The app is educational and informational. It is not medical advice, treatment, therapy, crisis care, or a replacement for audiology, ENT, mental health care, sleep medicine, or emergency care.

## Privacy

MVP data is local-only. Do not transmit entries, preferences, responses, journal content, reminders, My Plan items, or exercise records.

Do not add:

- accounts
- login
- remote analytics
- cloud sync
- clinician dashboards
- automatic export
- remote push notifications

## Crisis / unsafe thoughts

The app should never encourage self-guided exercises as a substitute for crisis help. It should communicate that if the user feels unsafe or is in a mental health crisis, the appropriate next step is immediate help, not continuing the app.

## Hearing care

The app should emphasize that direct hearing healthcare matters. Sudden hearing changes, unilateral symptoms, significant distress, pulsatile tinnitus, and other medical concerns should be evaluated by appropriate professionals. Hearing aids/amplification can be discussed as valuable sound therapy for people with hearing loss, but assessment belongs with audiology/ENT.

## Sound sensitivity

Do not encourage painful sound exposure. Sound sensitivity exercises use comfortable, controlled sound and nonjudgmental adjustment.

## Sleep

The app explains sleep/tinnitus relationships and starter tools. It does not provide full CBT-I/BBTI or diagnose/treat sleep apnea. Sleep apnea or serious sleep concerns should be addressed with appropriate care.

## Exercise and temperature tools

Temperature and movement tools are not meant to cause pain or injury. They are meant to create high-priority, high-contrast sensory input that helps attention shift.
